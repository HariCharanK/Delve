# Cooking Recipes

## Pasta Aglio e Olio

Simple Italian pasta that's incredibly flavorful.

Ingredients:
- 400g spaghetti
- 6 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 1/2 cup extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tsp red pepper flakes
- Fresh parsley, chopped
- Parmesan cheese

Instructions:
1. Cook pasta in salted water until al dente
2. While pasta cooks, heat olive oil on medium-low
3. Add garlic slices, cook until golden (NOT brown!)
4. Add red pepper flakes
5. Toss pasta with garlic oil, add pasta water as needed
6. Finish with parsley and parmesan

## Overnight Oats

Perfect breakfast that requires zero morning effort.

Ingredients:
- 1/2 cup rolled oats
- 1/2 cup milk (or oat milk)
- 1/4 cup Greek yogurt
- 1 tbsp chia seeds
- 1 tbsp honey or maple syrup
- Fresh berries

Instructions:
1. Combine oats, milk, yogurt, chia seeds, and sweetener
2. Stir well, cover, refrigerate overnight
3. Top with fresh berries in the morning
4. Can be stored for up to 3 days

## Chicken Tikka Masala

A winter comfort food classic.

Ingredients:
- 500g chicken thighs, cubed
- 1 cup yogurt (for marinade)
- 2 tbsp tikka masala spice blend
- 1 can coconut milk
- 1 can crushed tomatoes
- Basmati rice for serving

The interesting thing about tikka masala is that it's actually
a British invention, not traditionally Indian. It originated
in the curry houses of Glasgow or Birmingham in the 1960s.
